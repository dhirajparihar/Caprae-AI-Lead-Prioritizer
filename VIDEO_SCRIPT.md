# Caprae AI Lead Prioritizer - 2-Minute Demo Script

**Target Duration:** ~2:00 minutes  
**Tone:** Professional, direct, and operational (like a product walkthrough for a PE deal team).

---

### 0:00 - 0:25 | The Problem & Introduction
**Visual:** Screen recording starts on the empty, dense analyst workspace of the Caprae AI Lead Prioritizer. 
**Action:** Mouse moves to click "Upload CSV" and a dataset populates the table.
**Voiceover:** 
> "In private equity, the bottleneck isn't scraping data anymore—it's figuring out which three out of five hundred targets an analyst should actually spend their time on today. 
> 
> For the Caprae Capital AI-readiness challenge, I built a prototype that solves exactly this. It's not a generic SaaS dashboard, but a dense, operational tool built for a real analyst workflow."

---

### 0:25 - 0:55 | Thesis-Driven Scoring Engine
**Visual:** Focus on the "Screening" header. The user highlights the "Thesis" input bar. 
**Action:** The user types a new thesis: *"Founder-led B2B SaaS companies in the US under $50M revenue."* and clicks "Apply". The table instantly re-sorts.
**Voiceover:** 
> "Instead of rigid, hard-coded criteria, the tool is driven by an investment thesis. I can type a thesis in plain English. 
> 
> Behind the scenes, Google Gemini parses this thesis into structured, mathematical parameters. The deterministic scoring engine then instantly ranks the entire lead list based on this specific thesis. If the thesis changes, the pipeline instantly adapts."

---

### 0:55 - 1:25 | Transparent, Explainable Scoring
**Visual:** The user clicks on the top high-priority row in the table (e.g., Coastal Media). The right-side detail panel slides into focus.
**Action:** The mouse hovers over the "Score breakdown" and "Evidence" sections.
**Voiceover:**
> "A score is useless if it's a black box. The interface is built for explainability. 
> 
> When we select a target, the detail panel shows a transparent breakdown of exactly why a company scored an 85 out of 100. We can see precisely which data points matched our thesis, like revenue and geography, and where the target fell short."

---

### 1:25 - 1:50 | Actionable AI Insights & Outreach
**Visual:** The user clicks the "Generate note" button inside the AI Analyst section. 
**Action:** The AI note populates. The user scrolls down to see the "Next action" and the "Draft communication" text box. They click "Copy".
**Voiceover:**
> "But we don't stop at just scoring. With one click, we can generate an AI analyst note. 
> 
> Gemini synthesizes the target's data against our thesis, highlighting potential concerns and suggesting the strongest angle for outreach. It even drafts a highly personalized cold email to the founder, which we can edit and copy straight to our clipboard."

---

### 1:50 - 2:00 | Conclusion
**Visual:** Zoom out to show the full workspace with the selected target and the generated email.
**Voiceover:**
> "By bridging transparent, deterministic scoring with the natural language flexibility of LLMs, we get the best of both worlds: speed, explainability, and actionable insights. Thank you."
